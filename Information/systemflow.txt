BIZWOKE REVIEW MANAGEMENT SAAS - COMPLETE SYSTEM FLOW & ROLE ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

1. CORE CONCEPT & ROLES (Bizwoke vs Client Business)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Yeh ek B2B SaaS (Software as a Service) Platform hai jise "BIZWOKE" ne banaya hai.
Isme 4 alag-alag types ke log/roles hote hain:

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. BIZWOKE (SUPER_ADMIN)                                                    │
│    -> Aap (Platform Creator / SaaS Owner / Company Admin).                  │
│    -> Pure system ka master control aapke paas hai.                         │
│                                                                             │
│ 2. CLIENT BUSINESS (BRANCH_ADMIN) - e.g. "Kashish's Business", "Harsh ENT"  │
│    -> Yeh log aapke customers hain jinhone Bizwoke ka subscription buy       │
│       kiya hai ya 7-Day Free Trial use kar rahe hain.                       │
│    -> Yeh apna Google Review grow karna chahte hain aur bad reviews se      │
│       apne business ko bachana chahte hain.                                 │
│                                                                             │
│ 3. BRANCH USER / STAFF (BRANCH_USER)                                        │
│    -> Client Business ke employees, receptionists, counter billing staff.   │
│    -> Yeh sirf reviews dekh sakte hain ya QR/WhatsApp share bhej sakte hain.│
│                                                                             │
│ 4. END CUSTOMER (Patient / Diner / Visitor)                                 │
│    -> Jo counter par aakar QR code scan karta hai aur star review deta hai. │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

3. COMPLETE END-TO-END FLOW (Kaam Kaise Karta Hai?)
================================================================================

STEP 1: CLIENT ONBOARDING (Aapke Customer ka Signup)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Client (e.g. Kashish's Business) aapki website par aata hai aur Register karta hai.
2. Form me apna Business Name, Category, Phone, aur Google Review Link fill karta hai.
3. Account bante hi system use automatically:
   - "Free Trial (7 Days)" plan assign karta hai (100 reviews quota).
   - Ek unique review URL generate karta hai: e.g. /wtr/kashis82391
   - Client ka Role "BRANCH_ADMIN" ban jaata hai.

STEP 2: STANDEE & QR CODE GENERATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Client apne portal me "Share & QR" page par jaata hai.
2. Waha photorealistic Acrylic Counter Standee ready milta hai:
   - Header: "Kashish's Business"
   - Smiling Hearts & Modern UI: "We would love to hear from you."
   - Center QR Code: High-density scannable QR jiske beech me unka uploaded 
     Company Logo embedded hota hai.
   - Footer: "Powered by Bizwoke" (Bizwoke ki branding).
3. Client "Download High-Res Standee" par click karta hai:
   - 1200 x 1800 px (300 DPI) print-ready PNG download hota hai.
4. Client ise print karke apne clinic/restaurant ke reception counter par rakh deta hai.

STEP 3: CUSTOMER VISIT & SCANNING (Smart Review Gate)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Receptionist ya Table par baitha customer standee ka QR code scan karta hai.
2. Customer ke phone me Kashish's Business ka Live Review Page open hota hai:
   "How was your experience at Kashish's Business?"
   [ ★ ★ ★ ★ ★ ] (5 Smileys)

3. SMART REVIEW FILTERING (Most Important Business Value):
   
   A) AGAR CUSTOMER NE 4 YA 5 STARS DIYE (Happy Customer):
      -> System use turant redirect kar deta hai Client ke official Google Maps
         Review link par!
      -> Happy customer Google par 5-star review aur photo post karta hai.
      -> Result: Client ka Google rating & SEO rank upar chala jaata hai!

   B) AGAR CUSTOMER NE 1, 2 YA 3 STARS DIYE (Unhappy Customer):
      -> System use Google par NAHI bhejta!
      -> Ek private feedback modal open hota hai:
         "We are extremely sorry! Please let us know what went wrong so our 
          management can fix it immediately."
      -> Customer ka complaint, phone number aur reason private save ho jaata hai.
      -> Result: Bad reviews Google par public hone se bach jaate hain!

STEP 4: CLIENT DASHBOARD & NOTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Kashish's Business apne portal me Dashboard kholta hai.
2. Real-time updates:
   - 5-Star reviews count badh gaya.
   - "Negative Alerts" me 1 alert aaya jisme customer ka phone aur shikayat hai.
3. Business owner turant customer ko call karke issue resolve karta hai aur unhe
   loyal customer bana leta hai.

STEP 5: SUBSCRIPTION & BIZWOKE REVENUE (Monetization Flow)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Free Trial me client ko 100 reviews milte hain.
2. Jab 100 reviews complete hote hain ya 7 din pure hote hain, client ke portal me
   "Review Quota Exceeded / Trial Expired" alert aata hai.
3. Client "My Subscription & Upgrade" page par jata hai.
4. Client ko Bizwoke ke packages dikhte hain:
   - Starter Plan: ₹999/month (500 reviews, 1 location)
   - Growth Professional: ₹1499/month (1000 reviews, 1 location)
   - Enterprise Supreme: ₹2999/month (2500 reviews, multi-location)
5. Client online pay karta hai (Razorpay/Stripe ke through) aur plan active ho jaata hai.
6. Bizwoke ko monthly recurring revenue (MRR) milta hai!


================================================================================
4. TECHNICAL SECURITY & DATA ISOLATION (Kashish vs Other Clients)
================================================================================
- Multi-Tenancy Data Isolation:
  Kashish's Business sirf apna data, apne reviews aur apne staff dekh sakti hai.
  Kisi aur hospital ya hotel ka data Kashish's Business ko bilkul nahi dikhega.

- Role Separation (RBAC):
  1. SUPER_ADMIN (Bizwoke): Sabhi packages, users, settings aur global stats.
  2. BRANCH_ADMIN (Client Owner): Apne reviews, standee, staff aur subscription.
  3. BRANCH_USER (Staff): Only review check and customer invitation.
